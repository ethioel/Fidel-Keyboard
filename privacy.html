ፊደል Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Smart Amharic (Ge'ez) input method for the browser

The short version

ፊደል Keyboard does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no server, no analytics and no account. It makes no network requests at all. Anything it remembers — your settings, your snippets, your clipboard history and the words it learns from you — is stored in your browser's own extension storage on your own device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no usage tracking, no advertising identifier and no fingerprinting. The extension contains no code that sends data anywhere.

The only network access in the entire package is reading the Ge'ez word list, which is a file bundled inside the extension and loaded from the extension's own local URL (chrome.runtime.getURL("data/dictionary.json")). No request ever leaves your machine.

3. What syncs through your browser account

If you are signed in to your browser profile, Chrome may sync extension storage through your own browser account. This is a browser feature, not something the extension controls, and the data is never sent to the extension's author or to any third party.

The following is included in that sync:

  • Your settings — theme, typing mode, whether the on-screen keyboard opens automatically, sound, trace typing, and so on.
  • The hostnames of websites you added to your per-site allow or deny list. We do not record pages you visit; this is only the short list of domains you chose to save yourself.
  • Per-site language memory, if you enable it — the domain and the language you last used there.
  • Your learned words, but only if you turn on “Learn my typing”. If learning is off, nothing about your typing is recorded.

Your clipboard history and your snippets are never synced. They stay on the single device where you created them.

You can turn off all learning, and clear learned words and clipboard history at any time from the extension's options page. Clearing learned words removes them from both this device and your synced storage.

4. Clipboard history in particular

While the extension is enabled, it listens for the copy and cut events on pages and keeps the text involved, up to the most recent 20 items. This is how the clipboard feature works.

  • It captures text copied on any website, not only Amharic ones.
  • It never sends it anywhere, and no website can read it back.
  • It is stored only on this device.
  • Turning the extension off in the popup stops the capture immediately.

5. Permissions, and why each one is needed

6. Pages you visit

The extension does not keep a history of the pages you visit, does not read their contents, and does not send anything about them anywhere. Its only interaction with a page is inserting the text you type into the field you are typing in.

7. Children

The extension is a general-purpose typing tool and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights and controls

Everything the extension stores lives in your browser and can be removed by you at any time:

  • Turn the extension off entirely from the popup or from chrome://extensions.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.
  • Clear learned words, or reset the keyboard position, from the popup.
  • Clear clipboard history and edit or delete snippets in the popup.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the extension and its version number updated. Because the extension does not collect data, there is no separate notification process and no data to migrate or delete.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a privacy concern, can be raised through the issue tracker on this project's GitHub page.
